Böxli 2

Created in 2021
Amplifier: 2x50W R/L and 100W Bass based on the TPA3116. With Bluetooth 🤑
Batteries: two 4Ah 20V Li-ion (power tool) batteries. Standart Voltage inside the böxli is 20V. The Batteries can be charged with a 12V Power supply, because there is a step-up inside the böxli.
LEDs: based on the WS2812b with Arduino Nano controller.
\

VU Meter LCD: Velleman K8098 diy-kit.
